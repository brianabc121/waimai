隐私政策

生效日期：填日期

本政策说明 你的公司或门店名称（下称“我们”）在点餐网站和“员工工作台”应用中收集哪些信息、用来做什么、保存多久。联系我们：邮箱或电话。

1. 顾客

信息用途保存
姓名、手机号（短信验证码验证）确认订单是本人下的，配送时联系你，发送订单状态短信随订单保存，用于对账和售后
配送地址、备注，以及你在地图上标的位置（如果门店启用了地图）计算配送费，把餐送到随订单保存
订单内容和金额制作、配送、收款、对账按当地财税要求保存

配送途中，负责这一单的骑手能看到你的姓名、电话和地址；送达后骑手端不再显示你的电话和门牌备注。你能在订单页看到骑手的姓名、工作电话，以及他在取餐后到送达前的位置。我们不做线上支付，不收集银行卡信息。

2. 员工（使用“员工工作台”的店内员工和骑手）

信息用途保存
姓名、登录账号、手机号、岗位登录，分配任务，顾客和门店联系骑手在职期间；删除账号后，历史订单上只保留姓名
位置：只在你点了“上班”、并且处于骑手身份时采集，包括应用在后台或锁屏时让门店知道谁在岗、离店多远以便派单；让顾客看到自己那一单的骑手在哪最新位置在下班时清除；历史轨迹保存 7 天后自动删除
考勤：你每次上班、下班的时间；点上班或下班那一刻的位置（只取一次，可以不授权）；你做好了哪些菜、打包了哪些订单记录工时，统计工作量；让店长知道打卡时是否在店里在职期间长期保存。店长修改你的考勤时必须填写原因，原始记录保留
推送令牌（手机或浏览器的推送标识）发送新配送单、改派、取消的提醒退出登录或卸载后删除
送达照片（选填，由骑手拍摄）放门口或代收时留证，处理投诉30 天后自动删除
手机电量（仅随位置一起上报）门店判断骑手是否可能失联只保留最新一次的数值，不保留历史
你在系统里的操作记录（接单、取餐、送达、收款等）对账，处理纠纷随订单保存

点“下班”、退出登录，或连续 12 小时没有下班时，位置采集自动停止。不上班时我们不采集位置。位置只有本门店的店主、店长、收银能看到；顾客只能看到自己那一单配送途中的位置。

3. 我们使用的第三方服务

  • Google 地图：显示地图和搜索地址时，你的浏览器会直接访问 Google。
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging（Google）和各浏览器厂商的推送服务：传递推送提醒。提醒内容只有单号和地址。
  • 短信服务商 填写你用的服务商，例如 Twilio：发送验证码和订单状态短信，会收到你的手机号和短信内容。
  • 服务器托管 填写托管商和所在地区。

我们不出售个人信息，不用于广告。

4. 你的权利

你可以联系我们查询、更正或删除你的个人信息，或撤回同意。员工可以在手机的系统设置里随时关闭定位和通知权限；关闭后仍可使用工作台，但门店看不到你的位置，也收不到锁屏提醒。法律要求保存的记录（例如交易记录）在保存期满前不能删除。

5. 安全和变更

数据通过 HTTPS 传输，密码经过单向加密保存。本政策有变化时，我们会更新本页并修改生效日期。

Privacy Policy

Effective: date

This policy explains what your company or store name ("we") collects in the ordering website and the Staff Workbench app, why, and for how long. Contact: email or phone.

1. Customers

We collect your name and mobile number (verified by a text code), your delivery address, notes and, where the store uses a map, the point you mark on it, and the contents and amount of your order. We use them to confirm, prepare, deliver and account for your order, to contact you about it and to send order status texts. They are kept with the order as tax and accounting rules require. During delivery the assigned rider sees your name, phone and address; after delivery the rider no longer sees your phone or unit notes. You can see the rider's name, work phone and, between pickup and delivery, position. We take no online payments and collect no card data.

2. Staff (in-store staff and riders using the Staff Workbench)

  • Account: name, username, phone and posts, to sign in, assign work and let customers and the store reach a rider. Kept while you work with us; after deletion only your name remains on past orders.
  • Location: collected only after you tap "Start shift" and while you are in the rider identity, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. It lets the store see who is on duty and how far away for assigning deliveries, and lets a customer see the rider carrying their own order. Your latest position is erased when your shift ends; the track history is deleted after 7 days. Collection stops when you end your shift, sign out, or after 12 hours without ending the shift. No location is collected off duty.
  • Attendance: the times you clock on and off; your position at the moment you tap the switch (taken once; you may refuse it); which dishes you prepared and which orders you packed. It is used to count hours and work done, and to tell the manager whether you clocked on at the store. Kept while you work with us. A manager who corrects your attendance must give a reason, and the original record is kept.
  • Push token: to send reminders about new, reassigned and cancelled deliveries. Deleted when you sign out or uninstall.
  • Delivery photo (optional, taken by the rider): proof when an order is left at the door; deleted after 30 days.
  • Battery level, sent with the position, and a record of your actions in the system (pickup, delivery, cash collected), kept with the order.

Positions are visible only to the owner, managers and cashiers of your store; a customer sees only the rider of their own order while it is on the way.

3. Third parties

Google Maps (your browser contacts Google when a map or address search is shown); Firebase Cloud Messaging and browser vendors' push services (reminders carry only an order number and address); the SMS provider name (receives phone numbers and message text); hosting by provider and region. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Your rights

Contact us to access, correct or delete your personal data or to withdraw consent. Staff can turn off location and notification permissions in the phone's settings at any time; the workbench keeps working, but the store will not see your position and you will not get reminders on a locked screen. Records the law requires us to keep cannot be deleted before that period ends.

5. Security and changes

Data travels over HTTPS and passwords are stored hashed. When this policy changes we update this page and its effective date.
